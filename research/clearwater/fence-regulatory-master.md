# Clearwater Fence Regulatory Master

## Research status and method

This document directly reconciles `fence-regulatory-inventory.md` and
`fence-regulatory-reconciled.md` against the two supplied context extracts. It is
a working research consolidation, not a complete statement of current Clearwater
law and not a production rule package. Embedded source references below (for
example, “first-pass context 25132–25137”) are the original source line numbers
preserved inside the context extracts, not line numbers in this Markdown file.

The extracts mix supplement vintages and omit the substantive companion
Clearwater Community Development Code. A statement that a rule is supported by
an extract does not establish that the text is current as of the research date.
No missing dimension, definition, exception, or applicability condition is
inferred.

**Reconciliation labels**

- **BOTH** — substantively present in both prior documents.
- **INVENTORY ONLY** — present only in the inventory.
- **RECONCILED ONLY** — present only in the reconciled document.
- **RESOLUTION** — conclusion supported clearly enough by the supplied extracts.
- **UNRESOLVED — FULL SOURCE REVIEW REQUIRED** — the extracts cannot safely
  resolve scope, currency, meaning, or an apparent disagreement.
- **FALSE POSITIVE / REMOVE** — must not be carried into fence rules.

## 1. Confirmed General Fence Rules

### 1.1 Barbed wire adjacent to a street, alley, or sidewalk — BOTH

- **Ordinance section/reference:** Code of Ordinances § 28.06, “Barbed wire near
  streets” (first-pass context 25132–25137).
- **Applicability:** A person building or repairing a barbed-wire fence within
  the city, when that fence is adjacent to a street, alley, or sidewalk.
- **Requirement:** Building or repairing that fence is unlawful.
- **Exact numeric thresholds:** None. “Adjacent” is not quantified.
- **Exceptions:** None stated in the operative sentence.
- **Cross-references:** None stated in the excerpt. Definitions, enforcement,
  later amendments, and other material rules remain unavailable.
- **Confidence:** **HIGH** for the quoted prohibition and its expressed scope.
- **Full-section review required:** **YES** for current codification, the meaning
  of “adjacent,” and any exception elsewhere; the operative sentence itself is
  complete in the extract.

**Resolution:** Both prior documents reach the same substantive result. The
inventory says that no additional source review is needed, while the reconciled
document requires review for currency and definitions. The latter is the safer
position. The rule is citywide only in the limited sense that the sentence says
“within the city”; it is **not** a prohibition on every barbed-wire fence in every
location and does not establish that barbed wire is permitted elsewhere.

### 7.1 Containerized-service waste receptacle enclosures — BOTH

- **Ordinance section/reference:** Code of Ordinances § 32.284(1)–(7), “Waste
  receptacle” (both extracts at embedded source 35558–35611).
- **Applicability:** A property/use-specific special regime for customers
  receiving containerized solid-waste service. Subsection (1)(a) excludes a
  population described through a garbled reference to “paragraph 5 subsection
  (b).” Subsection (2) additionally addresses sites whose use or improvement
  requires site-plan approval or approval of a minor change of use. The complete
  covered population cannot be reconstructed from the two-column extract.
- **Requirement:** Visible text supports all of the following for the applicable
  covered class:
  - provide an enclosure and maintain it so the waste receptacle is not visible
    from ground level;
  - provide a gate wide enough for easy front-end-loading refuse-truck access;
  - provide the supported access and pad dimensions below;
  - when site-plan or minor-change-of-use approval applies, also follow the solid
    waste/general services director’s rules for enclosure dimensions, materials,
    location, and other physical attributes;
  - do not complete final building inspection or issue a certificate of occupancy
    for a site that has not complied with § 32.284;
  - construct and maintain enclosures under Community Development Code
    § 3-201.D and City Engineering Index 701; and
  - obtain city permission approving location before placing a solid-waste or
    recycling container outside a building or structure.
- **Exact numeric thresholds:** **12 feet of unobstructed access** for the truck;
  a **six-inch-thick reinforced-concrete pad** sized for the receptacle. Neither
  number is a fence height or property-line setback.
- **Exceptions:** The extract omits or interleaves the operative lead-in to
  subsection (5). Fragments refer to compliant existing walls/fences/screening
  and “extreme hardship,” but do not safely establish who may grant relief, which
  requirements may be waived, or whether accessibility/location is nonwaivable.
- **Cross-references:** Complete § 32.284 (especially subsection (5)); § 32.321;
  Community Development Code § 3-201.D; City Engineering Index 701; current
  director-promulgated specifications; applicable building and safety codes.
- **Confidence:** **HIGH** for the visible requirements and numbers; **LOW** for
  covered classes, subsection (5), and incorporated physical specifications.
- **Full-section review required:** **YES**.

**Different interpretation resolved:** The inventory reconstructs subsection
(5) as a director waiver of some or all requirements except accessibility and
location. The reconciled document declines to reconstruct that relief scheme.
Because the source columns are interleaved and the lead-in is absent, the
inventory overstates what the excerpt supports. The master adopts the reconciled
document’s conservative treatment: **UNRESOLVED — FULL SOURCE REVIEW REQUIRED**.

**Source:** Code of Ordinances § 28.06.

> testing, replacement, and shutoff operation  
> EXCEPT no fence-specific alternative or numerical clearance is stated in the
> supplied excerpt.

Do not translate the “typically within ten feet” point-of-service description
