## Supplemental safeguard and recommended policy wording

**No absolute-area notice override is justified by this bounded evidence.** The owner directly reviewed the largest-area examples: Fairwood 845.10 sq ft, Drew 467.55 sq ft and Enterprise 365.90 sq ft, and found them negligible for prominent notices. An area-only override would re-alert those reviewed cases and many unit records; long narrow bands can have sizeable summed area. Conversely, neither low area nor narrow width proves that a particular project is unrestricted.

**Retain the existing source/human exclusion gates and add no new numerical geometry rule without authorization.** For any later revised snapshot, changed membership or changed geometry fingerprint, rerun the investigation and hold new interior/broad OS/R regions, conflicting human evidence, boundary disputes or source-quality anomalies for human interpretation before applying suppression. The pinned membership and frozen evidence are the present safeguard against extending this conclusion to uninvestigated parcels. Continue project-specific City confirmation where relevant; do not mark shared-land rights, density or legal boundary questions resolved.

Proposed final policy: “Within the specifically approved CLR-Z-003 MDR + OS/R membership, recommend the contextual prominent OS/R notice only when mapped OS/R coverage is **strictly greater than 6%**. At **6% or below**, do not show a prominent split-zoning notice solely because of that intersection. This is presentation-only: retain MDR starting guidance, secondary mapped OS/R, coverage, geometry, viewer access, original REVIEW/source QA and provenance. Suppression does not erase zoning applicability, imply OS/R governs the whole parcel, waive restrictions, resolve shared-land/density/boundary questions, or override human/source-quality exceptions.”

The existing contextual notice text can remain for cases that warrant it; no alternate resident-facing wording or UI is implemented. The implementation scope, if subsequently authorized, is **notice metadata only on the same 731 approvals**, preserving all 1,967 Rule 001 approvals (>2% P), 262 Rule 002 approvals (>3% OS/R), classifications, exclusions and provenance. All 731 would have non-prominent recommendations; zero would have prominent recommendations in this frozen cohort. **Explicit owner approval of the 6% notice policy is still required.**
