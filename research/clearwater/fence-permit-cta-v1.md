# Clearwater fence permit CTA verification (Beta V1)

## Verification result

An authoritative resident-facing destination for beginning a Clearwater fence permit application could not be verified on August 26, 2026. No resident CTA should be published until the destination and its current application flow can be checked directly in a City of Clearwater source.

## Authoritative page found

None verified.

- **URL:** Not verified
- **Page title:** Not verified
- **Destination type:** Not verified (fence-specific application versus general permit portal)
- **Fence-specific application:** Not established
- **Residential fence permit type:** Not established from an accessible City source
- **Can a resident begin from the page?:** Not established
- **Account or login required:** Not established
- **Documents or prerequisites:** Not established

## Research performed

Research was limited to City of Clearwater domains and attempted to locate a fence-specific application first, then an official online permit portal or City permit instructions. Candidate City permit pages and the `epermit.myclearwater.com` host could not be retrieved in the verification environment: direct HTTPS requests were rejected by the network proxy before the City pages loaded. Internet search access likewise did not return authoritative page content that could establish the current page title, fence permit type, start action, login requirement, or prerequisites.

The existence or apparent naming of a City-hosted URL is not enough to satisfy this task. Without reading the current City page and following its resident-facing application route, the destination could be obsolete, could serve only existing applications, or could lead to a general landing page that does not support starting a residential fence permit.

## Important limitation and missing source

Verification still requires access to a current City of Clearwater page that does all of the following:

1. identifies the permit or record type used for a residential fence;
2. links to, or itself provides, the resident workflow for starting that application; and
3. states or visibly demonstrates whether account creation, login, and any material submission prerequisites are required.

Until that source is available, Groundrule cannot truthfully choose among “Apply for fence permit,” “Start permit application,” or “Open Clearwater permit portal,” and no application URL should be added to structured guide metadata or the resident UI.

## Resident-facing CTA recommendation

Do not add a CTA in this version. Keep the existing permit answer as **Required**. Re-run verification against a directly accessible City of Clearwater permit page or portal before adding resident action metadata.
