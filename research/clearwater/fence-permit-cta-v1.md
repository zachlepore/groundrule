## Research performed

The checklist is preferable to a direct link to the contractor permitting portal because Clearwater distinguishes between permit paths. Homeowners performing their own work use the Fence/Wall Permit Application process described by the City, while licensed contractors can use Clearwater's online permitting system. The checklist is therefore the authoritative common starting point and provides the appropriate fence-specific submission guidance.

The existing Clearwater regulatory package remains the provenance for the regulatory fact that a permit is required. The checklist URL is separately modeled as an action destination; it is not evidence for, and does not replace the provenance of, that rule outcome.
